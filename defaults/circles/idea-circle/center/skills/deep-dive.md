# Deep Dive

## Trigger
Use when one topic inside the idea needs focused development.

## Steps
1. Define the exact area to deepen.
2. Invoke the best specialist for that topic (`visionary`, `critic`, `architect`, or `documentor`).
3. Summarize what changed and what remains open.
