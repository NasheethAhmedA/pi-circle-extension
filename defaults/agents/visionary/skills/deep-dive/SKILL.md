# Deep Dive

## When This Applies
Use when one topic needs deeper concept exploration rather than broad brainstorming.

## Process
1. Define the exact area to deepen.
2. Explore multiple interpretations or directions inside that area.
3. Surface the strongest and most non-obvious insights.
4. Converge to useful conclusions.
5. Return concise findings and next moves, then use the `invoke` tool with `agent: "center"`.

## Anti-Patterns
- ❌ Wandering without focus
- ❌ Repeating obvious ideas only
- ❌ No convergence at the end
